# Learn

Deeper dives into how MeshCore and LoRa radio actually behave - field tests, measurements, and explainers from the CSRA community. You don't need any of this to get on the network, but it helps when you want to understand *why* a setup works (or doesn't).

Looking for what to buy? The [Hardware Guide](hardware.md) keeps things short and links back here for the details.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[RF Filters and Noise Floor](rf-filters.md)**

    Field test: a cheap 915 MHz bandpass filter near a water tower full of antennas took the noise floor from -92 back to -117 and the repeater count from 2 to 5. Also covers noise floor, RSSI, and SNR basics.

- **[RAK 1W Booster Power Test](diy-rak1watt.md)**

    Spectrum analyzer comparison of battery, USB, and external 5V power - and why USB alone can't keep the 1W amplifier at full output.

- **[MeshCore vs Meshtastic for EmComm](meshcore-vs-meshtastic.md)**

    A technical look at why MeshCore is the better fit for emergency communications, from routing and hop limits to delivery confirmation and airtime.
